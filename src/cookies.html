Cookie Policy

Welcome to Nimb-Netx! To make your immersion into the world of practical AI as effective as possible, ensure the stable operation of our digital infrastructure, and analyze interest in various technologies, we use cookies. This policy explains how we use these tools and how you can manage your user experience.

What are cookies?

A cookie is a small text file that is stored on your device (computer or smartphone) when you visit nimb-netx.org. This allows the platform to "remember" your preferences (for example, your chosen region or interface settings) so that you do not have to re-enter them when moving between the "Practices" or "Blog" sections.

What types of cookies do we use?

The Nimb-Netx platform uses the following categories of files:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Critical for the operation of the site. They provide secure connections and access to core features, such as submitting consultation requests via the contact form.
  • Analytical and Performance Cookies: Help us understand which AI tools and practices are most popular among everyday people. We collect anonymous data to improve the site structure and content quality.
  • Functional Cookies: Allow us to personalize your experience by remembering your settings, making every return to the site fast and convenient.
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to analyze the effectiveness of our offers in EU countries and deliver the most relevant information about innovations in the field of artificial intelligence.

Why do we use cookies?

The main goals of implementing these technologies on Nimb-Netx are:

  • Ensuring the secure operation of advanced digital infrastructure.
  • Analyzing interest in specific AI solutions to optimize our educational programs.
  • Content personalization: we strive to show you the practices that work specifically for you.
  • Statistical accounting of traffic from European regions to comply with local regulations.

Your choice and cookie management

You have full control over your data. You can change your settings or withdraw your consent at any time via your browser settings ("Privacy" or "Tools" section). Please note: disabling strictly necessary cookies may result in some interactive elements of the site and feedback forms becoming unavailable.

Third-party cookies

To analyze effectiveness, we may use partner tools (for example, Google Analytics). These services may set their own cookies to collect statistics on how AI technologies are changing the development approach of our users. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these services on their official resources.
