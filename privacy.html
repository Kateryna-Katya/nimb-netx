Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the personal data provided by the clients of the Nimb-Netx platform (nimb-netx.org), both for the provision of educational services in the field of AI and for other electronic interactions. We provide expert support at all stages and guarantee the protection of your privacy within our advanced digital infrastructure.

Nimb-Netx reserves the right to make changes and additions to the current provisions of the Policy. These changes will take priority over previous versions. Please note that our services may contain links to third-party resources that have their own rules. In such cases, Nimb-Netx is not responsible for the content of third-party policies. By providing your data, you open up new opportunities and give full consent to their processing by the methods described below.

Collection, Storage, and Destruction of Personal Data

Nimb-Netx is the Platform of the Year, which collects data only with your consent and exclusively to provide access to AI application practices, participation in innovative programs, and technical support. To turn dreams into reality in a safe environment, we use a full complex of information security measures accepted in EU countries.

The maximum storage period for personal data is 75 years from the date the information was received. In other cases, data is stored until the platform ceases its activities or until the user withdraws their consent.

Destruction or anonymization of data is performed to exclude the possibility of further processing once the purposes of collection have been achieved or upon client request.

Technical Information We Collect:

  • access time and device IP address;
  • sources of transition to the internet resource (referral links);
  • pages visited by the user in the "Practices" and "Blog" sections;
  • interaction with information blocks and AI tools;
  • technical data of the browser and operating system;
  • phone number (in case of a call initiation or request via the form).

When registering for access to the platform, we collect the data necessary for personalizing your learning experience and feedback:

  • username;
  • email address and phone number.

Information on User Actions:

  • data on agreements and contracts between the client and the platform;
  • information about requests sent to the support service;
  • data on mastered AI practices and tools used;
  • financial information related to payments in accordance with the laws of Germany and the European Union.

Purposes of Processing Personal Data:

  • implementing access to innovative technologies and AI tools;
  • accounting for preferences when developing new AI application practices;
  • informing about promotions, growth strategies, and special offers via e-mail and SMS;
  • providing prompt expert support on all matters.

Data Transfer to Third Parties:

Nimb-Netx does not provide your data to third parties, except for:

  • having your direct consent;
  • requirements of the law enforcement agencies of Germany/EU within the law;
  • cases of company merger or acquisition.

Use of Cookies and Beacons

Next-generation technologies require precise tuning. Cookies help us analyze traffic and save your preferences, making the start fast and easy. Pixel beacons are used for the graphical display of anonymized visit statistics. You can disable tracking in your browser settings at any time.

Your Rights

You can edit your data or request to stop its processing by contacting us at: support@nimb-netx.org. We strive to ensure that the use of our platform is transparent and secure.
