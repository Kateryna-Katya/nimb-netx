Personal Data Processing Policy

1. General Provisions

This Personal Data Processing Policy (hereinafter — the "Policy") defines the procedure and conditions for processing User data used by the administration of the Nimb-Netx platform (hereinafter — the "Operator") to ensure the operation of the project's advanced digital infrastructure.

1.1. The Operator's primary goal is to respect human rights and freedoms during the processing of personal data, ensuring the protection of the right to privacy and personal secrets in the process of mastering next-generation technologies.

1.2. This Policy applies to all information that the Operator may receive about visitors to the website nimb-netx.org.

2. Key Definitions

  • Website — a collection of graphic materials, software codes, and AI practices available at nimb-netx.org.
  • User — any visitor wishing to explore AI tools for real-world tasks.
  • Personal Data — information voluntarily provided by the User to receive expert support or access to growth programs.
  • Personal Data Processing — any actions with data (collection, storage, refinement, deletion) performed to ensure the quality of the platform's services.
  • Consent — a conscious expression of the User's will, confirmed through the feedback forms on the website.

3. Data We Process

As part of providing game-changing solutions, the Operator processes:

  • Profile Data:
    • Name (for identification in the system);
    • Email address (for delivering practices and innovations);
    • Phone number (digits only, for prompt support).
  • Technical Data (Cookies):
    • Anonymized data about the visit (IP address, device type, access time) collected for analytics on how technologies work for you.

4. Purposes of Processing

  • Providing the User with access to educational materials and the innovation blog.
  • Establishing feedback for free consultations regarding all aspects of AI application.
  • Informing about new passive income strategies and platform updates. You can always withdraw consent by writing to support@nimb-netx.org with the subject "Unsubscribe".
  • Improving the site structure so that the step-by-step path is clear to everyone.

5. Legal Grounds

The Operator processes data only with your voluntary consent, expressed by filling out the "Start Now" or "Request Access" forms. We operate within the framework of EU legislation, ensuring transparency and convenient adaptation without complexities.

6. Security and Data Storage

  • We take all necessary technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access. Your information is part of our trusted ecosystem.
  • User data is never shared with third parties, except in cases required by law or with the explicit consent of the User.
  • The data processing period is unlimited, but you have the "right to be forgotten". A request for data deletion can be sent to our Email.

7. Final Provisions

  • Any clarifications regarding the processing of your data are available through our support service: support@nimb-netx.org.
  • The current version of the Policy is always available on this page and remains in effect indefinitely. We build careers that work for you, based on the principles of honesty and data security.
